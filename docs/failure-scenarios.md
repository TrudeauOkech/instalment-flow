that prove it. Links go to the test methods.

Two scenarios behave differently from the design, and are listed as such:

- **16:** undoing an assignment is not built. The engine refuses nothing it should accept; it simply offers no
  reassignment command.
- **17:** settlement quotes are snapshots, not reservations.

The four scenarios of the original brief (duplicate callbacks, concurrent payments, reversal after settlement and
small payments) are `Scenario1` to `Scenario4` in the core tests.
